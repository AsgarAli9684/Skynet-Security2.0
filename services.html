What We Do

Six services. One security platform.

Every Skynet Security service runs on the same cloud-native control plane, so policies, telemetry, and enforcement stay consistent everywhere your business operates.

Zero Trust Network Access

Replace legacy VPNs with identity-aware access that connects users directly to the apps they need — never the network itself.

  • Continuous device posture checks
  • Application-level micro-segmentation
  • Adaptive, risk-based policies

Secure Web Gateway

Inspect every connection in line, decrypting and analyzing traffic at cloud scale to block threats before they reach a device.

  • Full TLS/SSL inspection at scale
  • Real-time malware and phishing defense
  • Granular web and app controls

Cloud Security

Continuously monitor cloud posture, workloads, and configurations to catch misconfigurations before attackers do.

  • Multi-cloud posture management
  • Workload and container protection
  • Automated remediation workflows

Data Protection

Classify sensitive data automatically and enforce consistent policy as it travels across devices, apps, and clouds.

  • Automated data classification
  • Policy enforcement across all channels
  • Insider risk detection

AI Threat Intelligence

Models trained on global traffic patterns identify emerging threats in real time and share protections instantly across the platform.

  • Behavioral anomaly detection
  • Predictive threat scoring
  • Shared intelligence across all tenants

Compliance & Reporting

Stay audit-ready with continuous reporting mapped to the frameworks that matter to your industry and customers.

  • Pre-built framework mappings
  • Real-time compliance dashboards
  • Exportable audit trails
