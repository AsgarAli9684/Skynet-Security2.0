Our Story

Protecting digital freedom in a connected world

Skynet Security was founded on a simple belief: enterprise security should be as intelligent and adaptable as the threats it defends against.

Our Purpose

Built by operators, for operators

We started Skynet Security after years spent inside SOC teams, watching good people drown in disconnected tools and alert fatigue. We set out to build the platform we always wished we had — one control plane, real intelligence, and answers instead of noise.

Today, that platform protects fast-growing teams who can't afford to choose between security and speed.
