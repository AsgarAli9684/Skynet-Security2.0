1. What are cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences, understand how visitors use it, and improve overall performance. We also use similar technologies such as pixels and local storage.

2. Types of cookies we use

We use the following categories of cookies on our website:

  • Essential cookies — required for the website to function, such as remembering your cookie preferences.
  • Performance cookies — help us understand how visitors interact with our site so we can improve it.
  • Functional cookies — remember choices you make to provide a more personalized experience.
  • Marketing cookies — used to measure the effectiveness of our campaigns and content.

3. Third-party cookies

Some cookies are placed by third-party services we use for analytics and marketing. These providers may collect information about your visits to our website and other sites over time. We do not control these third-party cookies directly, and we encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

4. Managing your preferences

Most browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings, including blocking or deleting them. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of our website. Where required by law, we will present a cookie consent banner allowing you to manage non-essential cookies before they are set.

5. Updates to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy periodically to reflect changes in the cookies and technologies we use, or for legal and regulatory reasons. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

6. Contact us

If you have questions about our use of cookies, please reach out via our Contact page or email us at privacy@skynetsecurity.example. See also our Privacy Policy for more on how we handle your information.
